# Experiment 001 Progress

Our initial objective was to demonstrate that a two-click foundation could be created entirely within the unmodified Sims 2 build engine.

We did not achieve this.

However, the attempt clarified the direction of the investigation.

The next stage of Experiment 001 will focus on extending an existing two-click foundation using only in-game tools. Understanding why extension succeeds may reveal the conditions required to create a two-click foundation from scratch.

## Major observations

Water interactions appear to influence terrain deformation.

Removing foundations may leave behind an invisible or persistent water state.

OBS recording configuration needs adjustment before future experiments.

Reflection

Although the original objective was not achieved, the experiment significantly refined the research direction.

Rather than attempting increasingly complex methods to create a two-click foundation directly, we will first investigate the mechanics that allow an existing one to be extended. A thorough understanding of the extension process may ultimately reveal how such foundations can be created entirely in-game.
