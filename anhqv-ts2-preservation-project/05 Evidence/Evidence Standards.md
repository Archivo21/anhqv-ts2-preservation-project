# Evidence Standards

Evidence is divided into several confidence levels.

## Primary Evidence

Gameplay recordings.

Raw screenshots.

Game files.

Package analysis.

Series footage.

Dialogue.

---

## Secondary Evidence

Community tutorials.

Forum discussions.

Developer interviews.

Existing documentation.

---

## Tertiary Evidence

Reasoned hypotheses.

Observed patterns.

Speculation.

These should always be clearly identified as such.

No hypothesis should ever be presented as an observed fact.
