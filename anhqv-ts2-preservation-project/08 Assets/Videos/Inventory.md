## Editing Rule

Raw evidence must be preserved unchanged.

Public edits should be exported as new files and accompanied by an edit log stating:

- the source recording or recordings;
- material removed or obscured;
- audio processing performed;
- timing changes;
- captions, annotations, or overlays added;
- whether any edit could affect interpretation of the evidence.

A polished video is a publication artefact. It does not replace the raw evidence from which it was produced.
