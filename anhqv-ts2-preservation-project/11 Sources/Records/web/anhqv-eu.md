## Description

ANHQV.eu is a long-running fan archive devoted to *Aquí no hay quien viva*. It combines encyclopaedic reference material, episode information, character and residence pages, continuity observations, production curiosities, physical-media analysis, fan writing, and community material.

The site is especially valuable because it preserves a large quantity of detailed ANHQV knowledge that might otherwise remain scattered across memory, old discussions, discontinued websites, and individual fan collections.

It should be treated as a structured map of research leads rather than as a canonical authority.

## Research Value

**Overall research value:** Very high  
**Discovery value:** Exceptional  
**Canonical authority without verification:** Limited  
**Structural value:** Very high  
**Preservation priority:** High

The site can help the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project:

- identify characters, residences, episodes, objects, businesses, recurring jokes, and secondary performers;
- locate continuity questions and possible production errors;
- reconstruct changes in occupancy and relationships across the series;
- identify terminology for a future ANHQV knowledge graph;
- discover claims requiring episode-level verification;
- locate press, DVD, production, and fandom material for further research;
- understand how committed fans have organised and interpreted the series;
- identify possible ANHQV specialists, consultants, or research colleagues.

The site is particularly useful as a bridge between informal fan knowledge and formal project research.

## Reliability and Verification Notes

### Source Status

ANHQV.eu is a **secondary fan source**.

It is valuable for discovery, orientation, terminology, and hypothesis generation. A statement appearing on the site should not be treated as verified solely because it is detailed or confidently written.

### Required Verification

Claims should be checked, where relevant, against:

- the broadcast episodes;
- episode credits;
- official releases and promotional material;
- contemporary press sources;
- interviews with cast or production staff;
- physical DVD evidence;
- other independently documented sources.

Repository records derived from ANHQV.eu should identify the exact page and access date.

### Mixed Content Categories

The site contains several epistemically distinct kinds of material:

1. descriptions of the broadcast series;
2. episode summaries;
3. editorial interpretation;
4. continuity observations;
5. production trivia;
6. physical-media analysis;
7. fan continuation;
8. invented episodes;
9. public comments and community discussion.

These categories must not be merged without labelling.

Fan continuation and invented episodes are useful records of fan culture, but they are not evidence of broadcast canon.

### Temporal Flattening

Some pages aggregate information across the entire run of the series.

For example, a residence page may list everyone associated with a flat without expressing the exact period during which each person lived there. Such information is valuable as a lead, but it must be converted into a dated or episode-bounded chronology before it can define a Sims household state.

### Internal Consistency

The site may contain:

- accumulated text written at different times;
- repeated or composite descriptions;
- claims lacking visible citations;
- outdated information;
- formatting or shortcode remnants;
- contradictory totals or classifications.

These are reasons to verify claims, not reasons to disregard the site.

### Attribution and Quotation

When the site prompts a research lead, the repository should prefer:

- concise paraphrase;
- page-level citation;
- independent verification;
- clearly labelled quotation only when necessary.

Substantial copying of prose should be avoided.

## Rights and Preservation Notes

Grupo Jonilar's legal notice states that original material published on its websites is made available under a **free-reading licence**, while rights remain with **Jonilar 21 SLU**.

The notice also states that material not wholly created by the operator may remain protected under the rights or licence of its original owner.

Therefore:

- linking and citing are permitted project practices;
- the site must not presently be treated as freely redistributable;
- a public repository mirror is not authorised by this source record;
- substantial reproduction should require explicit permission;
- permission from Jonilar 21 SLU may not cover every embedded third-party item;
- comments, photographs, video embeds, press scans, lyrics, and other third-party material may require separate treatment.

**Current project policy:** Catalogue and cite the live site. Do not publish a mirror or full archival capture in the repository unless permission and scope have been agreed with the operator.

## Proposed Project Use

ANHQV.eu should currently be used as:

- a discovery source;
- a research backlog generator;
- a source of terminology and entity names;
- a guide to likely continuity questions;
