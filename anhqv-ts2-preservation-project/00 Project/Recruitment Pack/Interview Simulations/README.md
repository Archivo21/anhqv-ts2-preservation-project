# Interview Simulations

Interview simulations test the project's procedure before a real candidate is involved.

A simulation must state clearly:

- that the subject is not a real candidate;
- what part of the process is being tested;
- what outcome language may be used;
- what claims cannot be inferred;
- how privacy and publication will be handled.

Simulations do not create contributor identities, roles, permissions, recommendations, acceptances, or rejections.
