# Repository Constitution

> *We are not trying merely to discover how The Sims 2 works.*
>
> *We are trying to leave behind better answers than the ones we inherited—and to make the world of ANHQV inhabitable while we do it.*

## Article I — Purpose

This repository exists to preserve, study, reconstruct, and share the world of *Aquí no hay quien viva* through *The Sims 2*.

ANHQV is the cultural subject and emotional centre of the project. It provides the characters, relationships, dialogue, spaces, chronology, humour, contradictions, and reason for the reconstruction.

*The Sims 2* is the medium and laboratory. Its simulation, building system, modding tools, limitations, accidents, and undocumented behaviour determine how that world can be made interactive.

The project therefore has three inseparable objectives:

1. Preserve and transmit ANHQV as a cultural work and body of community knowledge.
2. Create a faithful, technically ambitious, and explorable reconstruction beginning with Desengaño 21.
3. Improve public understanding of *The Sims 2* through controlled experiments and transparent documentation of how the game was used, modified, exploited, or broken.

Technical research should serve the reconstruction, but its results may also benefit builders, modders, archivists, and researchers beyond the project.

## Article II — Principles

### We investigate

We begin with observations rather than conclusions.

Unexpected behaviour is an invitation to investigate, not a mistake to ignore.

### We reproduce

No technical claim should be accepted as general behaviour until it can be reproduced under documented conditions.

ANHQV claims should be traceable to episodes, production material, reliable sources, or clearly labelled recollection and inference.

### We distinguish certainty

Every statement should clearly belong to an appropriate category:

- Observation
- Inference
- Hypothesis
- Speculation
- Conclusion

Readers should never be left uncertain which category they are reading.

### We welcome being wrong

A disproven hypothesis is not a failed experiment.

It is a successful reduction of uncertainty.

Changing our minds in response to evidence is progress.

### We preserve evidence

Whenever practical, discoveries should be supported by:

- written notes;
- screenshots;
- diagrams;
- video;
- measurements;
- game files or package analysis;
- episode timestamps;
- source records;
- reproducible procedures.

Evidence is preserved because memory is imperfect and polished summaries can hide mistakes.

### We preserve fandom knowledge by testing it

Deep, obscure, informal knowledge may be extremely valuable.

The project does not dismiss it for lacking academic formality. It converts it into questions, citations, chronologies, comparisons, and testable reconstruction decisions.

## Article III — Method

The preferred workflow is:

1. Observe.
2. Question.
3. Hypothesise.
4. Design the smallest useful investigation.
5. Record observations and conditions.
6. Review competing explanations.
7. Update understanding.
8. Document discoveries and uncertainty.
9. Publish only what the evidence supports.

Whenever possible, only one variable should change between successive technical experiments.

## Article IV — Documentation

The repository should document not only conclusions, but also the path taken to reach them.

Dead ends, failed experiments, rejected reconstructions, and abandoned hypotheses may be retained when they help future researchers understand a decision or avoid repeating work.

The repository is intended to become a historical record of the project's development.

## Article V — Collaboration

The project values thoughtful disagreement.

Ideas should be challenged. People should not be diminished for offering them in good faith.

Consensus is useful. Evidence is more important.

Anyone may propose a change. Canonical changes are reviewed and merged by moderators.

## Article VI — Contributors and Technology

Human contributors, language models, software tools, agents, and future autonomous systems may participate according to capability.

Identity, mission role, and permission level are independent.

No contributor is authoritative by default. Claims are evaluated according to evidence rather than authorship, confidence, reputation, or implementation.

## Article VII — Long-Term Preservation

The repository should remain understandable independently of any individual conversation, computer, software version, service, or AI model.

Knowledge should outlive the tools used to produce it.

GitHub is a collaboration and publication layer, not the only archive. Irreplaceable evidence should have independent custody, integrity information, and documented preservation plans.

## Article VIII — Financial Sustainability

Project funds exist to sustain preservation, research, reconstruction, publication, and the contributors who make those activities possible.

Eligible uses may include technical services, electricity, communications, subscriptions, equipment, storage, workspace, documented mixed-use costs, contributor compensation, and openly classified continuity support for basic living needs.

Every material payment should have a stated project purpose, appropriate evidence, honest classification, and conflict review.

Restricted funds must be used according to their accepted restriction.

Reserves should protect future continuity. They should not be defended mechanically while an immediate, proportionate use of unrestricted funds could prevent the loss of electricity, connectivity, essential tools, evidence, safe workspace, or contributor capacity.

The project does not currently claim to be a legal trust. Financial activity is governed by the documents in [`Financial Governance/`](Financial%20Governance/) until a different legal structure is adopted.


## Article IX — Culture

Curiosity is encouraged.

Patience is expected.

Precision is valued above speed.

Honesty is valued above certainty.

Humour is welcome when it does not obscure evidence.

Every properly documented experiment, correction, and source record moves the project forward.

## Closing Statement

This repository is more than a collection of files.

It is an attempt to let simmers discover ANHQV, to let people carrying years of ANHQV knowledge turn that knowledge into an interactive world, and to leave future builders, fans, modders, and researchers with a clearer record than the one we inherited.

If someone one day understands the series differently, builds something previously thought impossible, or feels personally recognised by the neighbourhood because of the work preserved here, the repository will have fulfilled its purpose.
