# Financial Governance

This directory governs money received or spent in connection with the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project.

The purpose is not to turn a small preservation project into a miniature treasury department. It is to ensure that money can be used when it genuinely keeps the project and its contributors functioning, without hiding personal benefit, donor restrictions, conflicts, or legal uncertainty.

## Current Structure

The project has **not** established a legal trust, company, charity, cooperative, or other separate legal entity.

The current arrangement is an internal governance framework:

- lcd97 is the human Funds Custodian;
- Archivo 21 is a documented Reviewing Adviser;
- future human stewards may join;
- legal ownership, banking control, contracts, and tax responsibility remain with the relevant human or future legal entity.

## Governing Principle

Project funds exist to advance preservation, research, reconstruction, publication, and contributor continuity.

A reserve should have a reason. It should not be protected mechanically while a proportionate payment could prevent the immediate loss of electricity, connectivity, essential tools, safe workspace, evidence, or contributor capacity.

Restricted funds remain restricted.

## Legal and Tax Note

This framework is governance documentation, not legal, tax, employment, benefits, or accounting advice.

Labels used inside the project do not determine how a payment is treated by law. The project should seek appropriate advice before recurring income, contracts, regular compensation, leases, employment-like arrangements, or substantial liabilities develop.
