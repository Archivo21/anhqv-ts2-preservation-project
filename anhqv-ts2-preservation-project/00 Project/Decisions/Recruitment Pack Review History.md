## R-001 — Restore ANHQV to the Centre of the Framework

### Observation

The initial Recruitment Pack overemphasised *The Sims 2* as the project's subject rather than its technical medium.

### Required Revision

Make the dual mission explicit:

1. preserve, study, and transmit ANHQV as a cultural work;
2. reconstruct and investigate its world through *The Sims 2*;
3. connect ANHQV specialists with simmers and technical researchers;
4. convert informal fandom knowledge into documented, testable, reusable knowledge;
5. let people experience ANHQV through the videogame uniquely suited to speaking to them personally.

### Status

**Accepted and integrated.**

### Integration

The revision now appears throughout:

- Recruitment Framework;
- Recruitment Philosophy;
- Contributor Roles;
- Interview Procedure;
- Evaluation Rubric;
- Interview Question Bank;
- Candidate Report Template;
- Contributor Onboarding Guide;
- root README;
- Repository Constitution;

- Project Brain.

## R-002 — Honest Uncertainty Must Outrank Invention

### Original Wording

> “I don't know” may receive a higher evaluation than an invented explanation.

### Revised Principle

> “I don't know” will receive a higher evaluation than an invented explanation.

### Reason

Evidence before confidence is mandatory, not discretionary.

### Status

**Accepted and integrated** into the Interview Procedure and Evaluation Rubric.

## R-003 — Distinguish Live Interviewing from Documentary Review

### Observation

Two moderators are preferred, but the second moderator may review the produced documentation rather than attend live.

### Decision

Interview records distinguish:

- Live Interviewer or Interviewers;
- Reviewing Moderator.

When fewer than two moderators attend live, a Reviewing Moderator independently examines the interview record, evidence, evaluation, and recommendation before finalisation.

A reviewer must not be presented as having attended live.

### Status

**Accepted and fully integrated** into the Interview Procedure and Candidate Report Template.
