**Version:** 0.1.0

## Purpose

The first public version establishes the project's identity, research method, governance, source system, and contribution routes before the playable neighbourhood or polished experiment media are released.

## Included

- project constitution, philosophy, culture, and roadmap;
- evidence standards and terminology;
- current experiment questions, notes, and draft field reports;
- Recruitment Pack 1.1;
- founder contributor archives;
- ANHQV.eu source record and source templates;
- public contribution and recruitment files;
- licensing, rights, media, and decision records;
- financial-governance framework and templates;
- Cleverbot mock-interview protocol for testing the interview process;
- links to three clean public viewing copies, then classified as EXP001.

## Not Included

- a playable neighbourhood release;
- raw MKV or AVI recordings;
- a completed EXP001 conclusion;
- a reconciled or final EXP001 evidence package;
- unreviewed third-party media or website mirrors.

## Public Message

This is a real research project at an early stage.

Questions, corrections, sources, experiments, and contributor interest are welcome. Claims remain evidence-dependent and subject to revision.

## Post-Release Corrections

The three built-in-recorder tests linked at release were later reclassified as EXP000. They test capture quality and do not establish a conclusion for the ongoing EXP001 two-click-foundation experiment.

The original Short remains embedded as public evidence on Archivo 21's EXP000 page. The other platform links are no longer listed publicly and may be shared directly with prospective editors.
