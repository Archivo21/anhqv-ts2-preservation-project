# Terminology

## Experiment

A reproducible investigation intended to answer a specific question.

---

## Observation

Something directly witnessed.

---

## Hypothesis

A possible explanation for one or more observations.

---

## Conclusion

A statement supported by sufficient evidence.

---

## Canon

Information explicitly established by Aquí no hay quien viva.

---

## Reconstruction

Information inferred from canon.

---

## Confidence

An indication of how certain a statement is.

🟢 Observed

🟡 Inferred

🔴 Speculative

---

## 2SF

Stands for Two Step Foundation. Commonly referred to in the community as a two-click foundation.
