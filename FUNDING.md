# Funding and Project Continuity

**Current public funding route:** None active  
**Current legal entity:** None established  
**Current governance:** Interim Project Funds Stewardship Committee

The ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project may eventually receive:

- YouTube or other platform revenue;
- donations and recurring supporter payments;
- grants;
- sponsorship;
- licence-compatible publication income;
- reimbursement for project work;
- other income connected with preservation, research, education, or community activity.

No donor or platform payment purchases authority over evidence, conclusions, contributor selection, or repository history.

## What Project Funds May Support

Project money may support the conditions required for the work to continue, including:

- electricity, internet, computing, storage, backup, hosting, and communications;
- software and service subscriptions, including access used to work with Archivo 21;
- recording, editing, preservation, accessibility, and publication;
- equipment, repairs, and technical services;
- dedicated workspace rent and operating costs;
- a documented project proportion of mixed-use household rent, utilities, or connectivity;
- food and drink connected with project sessions;
- contributor compensation, stipends, and continuity support when basic living costs would otherwise prevent meaningful participation;
- travel, accommodation, research access, and other evidenced project needs;
- purposeful reserves against future interruption.

Personal support is permitted, but it must be described honestly as contributor support or compensation rather than disguised as an ordinary operating expense.

## Before Activating Funding

Before a public donation or monetisation route is enabled, the project should establish:

1. an account or custody method;
2. a ledger;
3. a publication and privacy method for financial reports;
4. a process for restricted funds;
5. an appropriate review of tax, contractual, benefits, and legal consequences.

The project may revise its legal structure as activity grows.
