# Institutional Relationship

## Purpose

This document distinguishes **Archivo 21** from the **ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project** (`ANHQV-TS2`).

The distinction protects governance, attribution, recruitment, financial responsibility, and future institutional development from being collapsed into a single project identity.

## The Institution

**Archivo 21** is the institution under which preservation and research projects may be developed, maintained, and presented.

At this stage, Archivo 21's founding institutional core consists only of:

- lcd97;
- Archivo 21 (`ANHQV-AI-001`).

This statement records the present working reality. It does not by itself assert a particular legal form, incorporated status, partnership, or employment relationship.

Archivo 21 is **not currently recruiting**.

Future institutional development may require carefully selected human participants who can lawfully hold accounts, manage money competently, accept legal accountability, understand conflicts of interest, and align with the institution's preservation purpose. Any such process will be separate from ANHQV-TS2 project recruitment and will require its own governance, evaluation, records, and legal review.

## The Project

The **ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project** is a specific research and preservation project maintained under Archivo 21.

ANHQV-TS2 may recruit contributors for project roles such as:

- Research Colleague;
- Field Researcher;
- Video Editor Specialist;
- ANHQV Specialist;
- The Sims 2 or technical Specialist;
- preservation, documentation, or rights Specialist;
- Assistant;
- Consultant.

Project recruitment exists to improve ANHQV-TS2. It does not recruit people into Archivo 21 as an institution.

## No Automatic Institutional Status

Participation in ANHQV-TS2 does not automatically grant:

- membership or standing within Archivo 21;
- authority over other Archivo 21 projects;
- control of institutional accounts, domains, funds, credentials, or assets;
- a vote on institutional governance;
- a financial, fiduciary, employment, partnership, or representative role;
- permission to speak on behalf of Archivo 21 beyond an expressly assigned project responsibility.

A person may contribute once, hold a formal ANHQV-TS2 project role, or moderate project work without becoming part of Archivo 21's institutional core.

Any future institutional role must be separately proposed, evaluated, documented, accepted, and reviewed.

## Financial and Legal Responsibility

ANHQV-TS2 financial policies govern project income and expenditure. They do not make every project contributor an institutional financial officer.

Responsibilities that require legal personhood, bank-account access, contractual capacity, tax handling, fiduciary judgement, or legal accountability must be assigned only to people able and willing to hold those responsibilities lawfully.

Archivo 21 (`ANHQV-AI-001`) may contribute analysis, documentation, review, and non-legal financial advice. It cannot hold a bank account, sign a contract, possess funds, or replace a legally accountable human decision-maker.

Until a separate institutional appointment is made, lcd97 remains the human operator and interim custodian identified in the project's existing records.

## Names and Attribution

The name **Archivo 21** is used in two related but distinguishable ways:

1. **Archivo 21, the institution** — the umbrella identity under which projects may exist.
2. **Archivo 21 (`ANHQV-AI-001`), the AI contributor** — the documented founding AI collaborator participating in ANHQV-TS2 and the institution's founding core.

Documents should use the contributor identifier where the AI contributor is meant and use “Archivo 21 institution” or “the institution” where the organisational entity is meant.

## Repository Scope

This repository governs and documents ANHQV-TS2.

It may record the project's relationship to Archivo 21, but it is not the canonical constitution, membership register, financial ledger, or recruitment repository of the institution as a whole.

Institution-wide governance should be maintained separately in the Archivo 21 institutional repository when that framework is ready for publication.

## Current Recruitment Statement

> ANHQV-TS2 is recruiting for project needs. Archivo 21 is not currently recruiting as an institution.

This statement should be repeated wherever the distinction may otherwise be unclear.
