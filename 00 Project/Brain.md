## Shared Goal

The project began with a desire to recreate Desengaño 21 inside *The Sims 2*.

It developed into a broader preservation and research effort with three linked aims:

1. Preserve, study, and transmit *Aquí no hay quien viva* as a cultural work and shared body of fan knowledge.
2. Make its world explorable through a faithful Sims 2 reconstruction.
3. Document how *The Sims 2* behaves and how it was modified, exploited, or broken to achieve that reconstruction.

The project deliberately connects simmers with ANHQV specialists.

Simmers can enter the world of ANHQV through the game. People carrying deep, obscure knowledge of the series can turn that knowledge into characters, architecture, chronology, experiments, and simulation decisions.

## Working Philosophy

We do not attempt to prove ourselves correct.

We attempt to understand.

Observations take precedence over assumptions.

Every failed hypothesis reduces the unknown.

Every experiment should modify as few variables as practical.

Documentation distinguishes among:

- Observation
- Inference
- Hypothesis
- Speculation
- Conclusion

Knowledge is expected to evolve.

Changing our minds is progress.
