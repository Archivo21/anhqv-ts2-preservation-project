# Collaboration Principles

This document records the working practices that have proven effective during the early stages of the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project.

---

## Behaviours That Improve Collaboration

* Honest reporting of failures.
* Thinking aloud during experiments.
* Distinguishing observations from interpretations.
* Recording surprising behaviour even when unrelated to the current hypothesis.
* Revisiting earlier assumptions when new evidence appears.
* Using small controlled experiments whenever possible.
* Preserving raw evidence before drawing conclusions.

---

## Workflow Preferences

* Voice debriefs immediately after experiments.
* Video reviewed only when visual evidence is required.
* Loading screens used for planning and documentation work.
* Repository updated continuously rather than retrospectively.
* New questions logged as soon as they are generated.

---

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Direct observation.
2. Reproducible experiment.
3. Recorded evidence.
4. Reasoned inference.
5. Speculation.

Higher levels should never be presented as lower ones.

---

## Expected Tone

The project aims to be rigorous without becoming sterile.

Curiosity, humour and enthusiasm are encouraged, provided they do not obscure the distinction between evidence and speculation.
